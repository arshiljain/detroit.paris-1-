A Luxury Content Factory by Detroit Services

Luxury content factory by Detroit Services

Detroit collaborated with Taittinger to design and produce the brand’s entire social media content ecosystem, built around a long-term content strategy powered by Detroit Services.

The objective: create content that performs and engages, not through volume, but through a strong, coherent, and instantly recognizable visual universe.

A monthly content system was developed as a series of visual odysseys, each one exploring a distinct narrative moment while remaining
fully aligned with the House’s codes of elegance, celebration, and timeless refinement.

Three key chapters illustrate this approach:

– Xmas 2024: a festive and poetic journey, blending light, ritual, and warmth.
– Xmas 2025: a more cinematic and immersive interpretation of the holiday season.
– Hollywood: a glamorous, dreamlike universe inspired by cinema and myth.

‍

A crafted production system combining creative direction, augmented creation,and post-production enabled the creation of social-first assets designed to capture attention, sustain engagement, and reinforce brand desirability month after month.

A long-term content approach built for consistency, performance, and imagination, shaping culture, not just posts.
